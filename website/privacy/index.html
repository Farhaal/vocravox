Privacy

Vocravox runs on your own computer. There is no account and no tracking.

What stays on your computer

  • Your audio files and the videos you make.
  • Your API keys (stored only on this computer).
  • Your projects, exports and the downloaded footage.

What leaves your computer, and to whom

WhatSent toWhy
Search words for each lineThe stock libraries you use (Pexels, Pixabay, Openverse, Wikimedia)To find footage
The text of your scriptThe AI providers you turn on (for example Google Gemini or Groq)To understand the topic and write searches
A check for new versionsThe download page on GitHubTo offer updates

The AI providers and stock libraries handle that data under their own privacy policies. Free AI plans, such as Google Gemini's free tier, may use what you send to improve their products; use a paid key if that matters for your project.

Vocravox never sends your audio, your keys, or your files to us.
