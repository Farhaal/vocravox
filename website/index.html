Install in a few minutes

  1. Download and run the installer

    It installs for your user only, so it never asks for administrator rights.

    Seeing “Windows protected your PC”? Vocravox is new and its installer isn’t signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only do this for a file you downloaded from this page.

  2. Let it set up once

    On first launch Vocravox detects your computer and downloads only the parts it can use, including the premium models, so your first video starts right away. Choose Full for the best clip matching, or Lite for a smaller download.

  3. Add your free keys

    In Settings, paste free keys for Pexels and Pixabay (video clips) and an AI provider such as Google Gemini. The in-app guide shows where to get each one.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, you can use the current version for free, including for videos you monetize. Future versions may add paid options; versions you already installed keep working.

What AI does it use?

Vocravox runs premium AI models on your own computer, together with its own matching engine. The models download once during setup, and your audio never leaves your PC.

Do I need a powerful PC or a graphics card?

No. It runs on any modern computer. A graphics card makes it faster, especially on long videos.

Which video editors work with it?

Any. The export is a folder of numbered clips, your narration and captions. DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro can also import a ready-built timeline.

Where do the clips come from?

Pexels, Pixabay, Openverse and Wikimedia Commons, limited to licenses that allow commercial use. Every export includes a credits file you can paste into your video description.

What leaves my computer?

Search words go to the stock libraries you use, and the words of your script go to the AI provider you turn on. Your audio, your keys and your projects stay on your computer. See the privacy page.

Why do I need my own keys?

They are free and keep your usage limits yours. Vocravox doesn’t sit in the middle, so there are no accounts and nothing to pay us.

Is there a Mac version?

An Apple Silicon version is planned. It isn’t available yet.
