Help

Install

  1. Download and run the installer. It installs for your user only, so it never asks for administrator rights.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway. The installer is new and not signed yet. Only do this for a file you downloaded from this site.
  3. Let it set up once. On first launch Vocravox downloads the parts your computer needs, including its premium models. Choose Full for the best results or Lite for a smaller download.
  4. Add your free keys in Settings: Pexels and Pixabay for footage, and an AI provider such as Google Gemini. The guide inside the app shows where to get each one.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, you can use the current version for free, including for videos you monetize. Future versions may add paid options; versions you already installed keep working.

Which video editors work with it?

Any. The export is a folder of numbered clips, your narration and captions. DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro can also import a ready-built timeline.

Where does the footage come from?

Pexels, Pixabay, Openverse and Wikimedia Commons, plus Wikipedia and NASA’s image library for real photos of named people, places and events, limited to licenses that allow commercial use. Every export includes a credits file you can paste into your video description.

What leaves my computer?

Search words go to the stock libraries you use, and the words of your script go to the AI provider you turn on. Your audio, your keys and your projects stay on your computer. See the privacy page.

Why do I need my own keys?

They are free and keep your usage limits yours. Vocravox doesn’t sit in the middle, so there are no accounts and nothing to pay us.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. It runs on any modern computer. A graphics card makes it faster, especially on long videos.

Still stuck?

In the app, choose Help, Copy diagnostics and send it to support@vocravox.com. Your keys are never included.
