How Vocravox
is built.

A desktop product that runs entirely on your computer, designed and engineered by one developer. Here is what is inside, and how well it holds up.

From voice to timeline

Seven steps, each one built for accuracy first.

  1. 1

    Timing

    Word-by-word timestamps from your real voice. Nothing is ever timed from the script.

  2. 2

    Pacing

    Words become scenes. Cut length follows where the line sits and the pace you choose.

  3. 3

    Understanding

    The whole script is read first, so every pick fits the story, not just a keyword.

  4. 4

    Search

    Licensed libraries searched in parallel, with automatic failover when one is busy.

  5. 5

    Judging

    Candidates are judged on your computer for how well the picture itself fits the line.

  6. 6

    Cutting

    Each clip starts at its best moment and is cut to the exact length of its words.

  7. 7

    Export

    Numbered clips, captions, a ready-made editor timeline and a credits file.

  8. Original engine

    Premium AI models do the perceptual work. The engine around them is original work: pacing, story understanding, judging, cutting and the timeline itself.

Measured, not guessed

The fastest way to export depends on the computer. So Vocravox times the real work on yours, once, and uses what is actually fastest there.

Built to keep going

Most of the effort goes into what happens when something goes wrong.

Falls back, never stops

A clip that fails one way moves to the next, and the last resort always works. A failing part never costs you the export.

Recovers on hard computers

If security software holds the video tool up, the app switches to a gentler mode by itself, or tells you exactly what to allow.

Never fakes success

Every clip is checked. Real progress, time left and a Cancel button; problems are counted and reported, not hidden.

Setup that never repeats

Models download once, resume after an interruption, and are never fetched again on an update or reinstall.

  • 250+automated tests, run on every change
  • 4stock sources, commercial-use licences only
  • 0accounts, servers or subscriptions
  • 1developer, start to finish

Private by design

Your audio, keys and projects stay on your computer. Only search words go to the stock libraries, and your script’s text goes to the AI provider you turn on. Read the privacy page.
