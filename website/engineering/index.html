Built to get
every clip right.

One developer, one promise: the footage fits the words. Here is how Vocravox keeps it, on your own computer.

A line about a winter blackout should never come back with outer-space footage.

Every faceless-video creator knows that moment. So Vocravox was built around one idea: understand first, then choose.

Listen

It starts with your voice.

Your real voice is the clock for everything that follows.

  1. Timing

    Every word gets its own timestamp, on your computer. Nothing is ever timed from the script.

  2. Pacing

    Words group into scenes. Cut length follows where the line sits in the video and the pace you choose.

Understand

Then it reads the whole story.

Context first. Footage second.

  1. Understanding

    The full script is read before a single clip is picked, so each choice fits the story, not just a keyword.

  2. Search

    Licensed libraries are searched in parallel, with automatic failover when one is busy or out of quota.

  3. Judging

    Candidates are judged on your computer for how well the picture itself fits the line. Nothing repeats, and the opening gets first pick.

Deliver

And hands it to your editor.

Keep the tools you already love.

  1. Cutting

    Each clip starts at its best moment and is cut to the exact length of the words it covers.

  2. Export

    Numbered clips, captions, a ready-made timeline for your editor and a credits file. Drop them in, in order, and they line up with your voice.

Premium models see and hear. The engine decides.

Pacing, story understanding, judging, cutting and the timeline itself are original work, built around those models.

Measured

Not guessed.

The fastest way to export depends on the computer, so Vocravox times the real work on yours, once, and uses what is actually fastest there.

Reliable

When something goes wrong, it keeps going.

Most of the effort goes into the moments you never see.

Falls back, never stops

A clip that fails one way moves to the next, and the last resort always works. A failing part never costs you the export.

Recovers on hard computers

If security software holds the video tool up, the app switches to a gentler mode by itself, or tells you exactly what to allow.

Never fakes success

Every clip is checked. Real progress, time left and a Cancel button; problems are counted and reported, not hidden.

Setup that never repeats

Models download once, resume after an interruption, and are never fetched again on an update or reinstall.

  • 250+automated tests, run on every change
  • 4stock sources, commercial-use licences only
  • 0accounts, servers or subscriptions
  • 1developer, start to finish

Private by design.

Your audio, keys and projects stay on your computer. Only search words go to the stock libraries, and your script’s text goes to the AI provider you turn on. Read the privacy page.
